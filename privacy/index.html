Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Couchpad does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks you.

How it works

Couchpad has two parts: an iPhone app and a free Mac app. They talk to each other directly over your own Wi-Fi network. Nothing you do in Couchpad, such as pointer movements, key presses or media controls, leaves your network or passes through any server of ours.

What stays on your devices

  • On your iPhone: the PIN you entered, the name of the Mac you last connected to, and your settings (dark mode, colour, brightness, pointer speed and which effects to show on the Mac).
  • On your Mac: the pairing PIN and whether the app opens at login.

This information is kept only on those devices and is removed when you delete the apps.

Typing

When you use the typing screen, the text you type is sent straight to your Mac over your Wi-Fi network and typed into the app you are using there. Unless you switch it off in Settings, the Mac also shows the text in large type on its screen for a moment. The preview is hidden while a password field is selected. Couchpad does not keep or send anywhere what you type.

Permissions

  • Local Network (iPhone): lets the app find and connect to your Mac on your Wi-Fi network.
  • Accessibility (Mac): lets the Mac app move the pointer and press keys when you ask it to from your phone.

The Mac app does not read the contents of your screen, your keystrokes or your files. Couchpad does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's own privacy statement.

Children

Couchpad is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@couchpad.app.
