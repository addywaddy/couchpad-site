Setup guide

Three steps: put the Mac app on your Mac, let it control the pointer and keys, then connect your iPhone.

  1. Install

    Download the Mac App, drag it to Applications and open it. It lives in your menu bar behind a couch icon.

  2. Permit

    Under Privacy & Security, allow Couchpad in Accessibility so it can move the pointer and press keys for your phone. The button in the menu takes you there.

  3. Connect

    Open the Couchpad menu to see your PIN, then enter it in the iPhone app and choose your Mac.

Your iPhone and Mac need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Brightness works on the screens macOS lets apps control, such as the built-in display and Apple displays. Many other monitors can only be adjusted with their own buttons.

Something not working? See Support, or write to support@couchpad.app.
