couchpad

Your Mac, from the couch.

Couchpad turns your iPhone into a glass trackpad, media remote and keyboard for your Mac. Play, pause, skip, type, change the volume and dim the screen without leaning over the laptop.

Free Mac app · macOS 13 or later · signed and notarized

Everything within thumb's reach

A real trackpad

  • Move, tap to click, tap with two fingers to right-click
  • Scroll with two fingers
  • Hold a finger still to drag windows or select text
  • Pointer speed you can adjust
  • An optional fading trail, and a soft ping where it stops, in any colour you choose, to find the pointer on your Mac's screen

Type from the couch

  • Use your iPhone's keyboard; the words appear on your Mac as you type
  • Drag along the space bar to move the cursor on the Mac
  • See what you are typing in large type on the Mac's screen

A proper remote

  • Play, pause and skip 10 seconds in your browser's video player and the Apple TV app
  • Volume, and a choice of speakers or headphones
  • Brightness for each screen, down to off
  • A fullscreen switch that stays in step
  • A dark mode for dark rooms: black with white or any colour you choose, and dim it below the iPhone's own minimum

Private by design

  • Talks to your Mac over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet
  • Paired with a PIN, and locks after wrong guesses
  • No account, no ads, no tracking

Set up in a minute

  1. Install the Mac app

    Download Couchpad, drag it to Applications and open it. It lives in your menu bar.

  2. Allow it to help

    Under Privacy & Security, allow Couchpad in Accessibility so it can move the pointer and press keys for your phone.

  3. Enter the PIN

    Open the Couchpad menu to see your PIN, then enter it in the iPhone app and choose your Mac.

Your iPhone and Mac need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Brightness works on the screens macOS lets apps control, such as the built-in display and Apple displays. Many other monitors can only be adjusted with their own buttons.
